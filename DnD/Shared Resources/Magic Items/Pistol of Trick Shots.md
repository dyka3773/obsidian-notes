## Properties & Effects
**Trick Shot.** At the start of each combat, roll a d6 to determine the pistol's effect until the end of that combat.

1. Explosive Shot. When you hit a creature with a ranged attack using this weapon, each creature within 5ft of the target takes 2 damage
2. Forceful Shot. When you hit a creature, you can push it up to 10ft away from you.
3. Ricochet Shot. When you miss an attack, you can reroll the attack against a different target within 10ft of the original target.
4. Guided Shot. Once on each of your turns, you can gain a +2 bonus to an attack roll you make with this weapon
5. Pinning Shot. When you hit a creature, its Speed is reduced by 10ft until the end of your next turn.
6. Flash Shot. When you hit a creature, you can force it to make a CON saving throw (DC=8+your PB+DEX). On a failed save, the creature is blinded until the start of your next turn.
