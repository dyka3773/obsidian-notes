## Properties & Effects
It is woven from pure luminous moonsilk.
While wearing it, you can cast the [[Light]] cantrip at will
**Gifted Healer.** If you are a Paladin, your Lay on Hands pool increases by 5 HP. This bonus increases by an additional 5HP for every 3 levels you have in the paladin class.
