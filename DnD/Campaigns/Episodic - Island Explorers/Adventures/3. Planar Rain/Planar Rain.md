## Session Summary
In the midst of their travels to their next island, during the night, a large rainstorm cloud appeared overhead.
Some of the party were led to the Underdark, some in the Feywild and some in the Astral Plane.
The ones in the Underdark and Feywild faced immediate dangers unique to their respective planes, while those in the Astral Plane encountered a strange and otherworldly figure who seemed to offer cryptic guidance about their journey.

## What Happened
### Scene 1: Storm at sea

The scene begins with the party sailing toward their next island, enjoying the calm and clear night sky.
Some of the party will probably be sleeping below deck when the rainstorm hits. 
Ask them who does what. Someone also needs to watch the helm, probably the party's navigator or captain.
> The night is unusually clear.
> The sea is calm, and the stars above are brighter than usual, there are more of them tonight than ever before.
> Then, without a warning, a single drop of rain lands on the deck.
> It glimmers like liquid silver.
> Another strikes the mast.
> Then a thousand more.

Roll for Perception.
1. Asleep:
   1. Lower perception -> Underdark [Scene 2a]
   2. Highest perception -> Astral Plane [Scene 2d]
2. Awake:
   1. \>12 -> Feywild [Scene 2b]
   2. <=12 -> Material Plane [Scene 2c]

### Scene 2d: Astral Plane
> You wake up from a nightmare you saw yourself falling infinitely into a void, the sensation of freefall still lingering as you try to regain your bearings.
> As you open your eyes fully, you see an aura of magic coming into your quarters.
> This aura is sparkling with white and gray light, casting shifting patterns across the room as it moves.
> You feel a strange pull towards the deck, as if an unseen force is guiding you there.

(If they try to interact with someone else they can't and that's when they also realize that they have the same aura on them)
(If they look behind, they see themselves sleeping calmly)

> As you walk outside, the aura seems to be coming from a tall, shimmering figure floating above the deck, radiating an ethereal light that dances across the waves.
> The sea around the ship is now a silvery liquid
> The ship itself is glowing softly with an ethereal light.
> Behind you a massive semi-translucent silver tree rises from the middle of the deck, right above the ship's Sanctuary, its branches stretching towards the sky and shimmering with an otherworldly light.
> The figure approaches slowly.
> It appears to be a spirit in a cloak of starlight, but it has no face.
> "You are not supposed to be here"
> It looks past you, towards the tree behind you.
> "No, perhaps you are exactly where you must be."
> Beyond the spirit, something enormous moves in the silver darkness. You cannot tell whether it is a creature, a ship, or the shadow of a thing that has no place among the stars.
> "The light is dying," it whispers, "and those who depend upon it have begun to harvest the living."
> It turns towards you
> "When the roots reach your world, you will have very little time."
> "Seek the one who knows the language of ships. She will show you the way."
> It reaches into its cloak and pulls out 4 scrolls, each glowing faintly with the same ethereal light that surrounds it.
> "These will aid your journey and help you navigate the perils that lie ahead."
> It hands them to you
> "Good luck, and may the stars guide your path."
> The spirit then slowly ascends into the sky, its form dissolving into the starlight, leaving you alone taking away all traces of its presence and the ethereal light it brought with it.
>
> You only now realize it is pouring with rain and that Z is next to you, helping X to fight off the strange creatures emerging from the silver darkness.
