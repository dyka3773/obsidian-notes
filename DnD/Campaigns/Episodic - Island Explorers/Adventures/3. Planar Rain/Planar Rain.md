## Session Summary
In the midst of their travels to their next island, during the night, a large rainstorm cloud appeared overhead.
Some of the party were led to the Underdark, some in the Feywild and some in the Astral Plane.
The ones in the Underdark and Feywild faced immediate dangers unique to their respective planes, while those in the Astral Plane encountered a strange and otherworldly figure who seemed to offer cryptic guidance about their journey.
