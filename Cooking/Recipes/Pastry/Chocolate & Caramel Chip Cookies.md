# Ingredients
For ~25 Cookies
- [ ] 140g butter
- [ ] 266g caster sugar
- [ ] 12g vanilla extract
- [ ] 2 Large eggs
- [ ] 1 tsp baking powder
- [ ] 1 tsp baking soda
- [ ] 1 tsp sea salt
- [ ] 272g flour
- [ ] 158g dark or couverture chocolate chips
- [ ] 158g Sahne Muh-Muhs cut as chips about the same sizes as the chocolate

# Directions
1. Mix the butter, sugar and vanilla until combined into a sugary butter paste using a stand mixer
2. Add the eggs, baking powder, baking soda and sea salt, and mix thoroughly
3. Add the flour and mix until homogenous
4. Add the chips/crumbles of chocolate and caramel and mix with a spoon or a spatula so that it is not destroyed
5. Preheat the oven at 170°C-180°C on air
6. Take 2 tablespoons or an ice cream scoop and form small balls of dough
7. Place each ball on a baking tray leaving ~5cm distance between them so that there's enough space for them to melt while cooking
8. Bake in the oven for about 11 minutes
9. After the 11 mins pass, take them out and while still hot, using your spatula or a ring form, shape the cookies into a more circular shape if needed
10. Back in the oven for 1 more minute
11. After taking them out the final time, allow them to cool down for 10 minutes to become more rigid. (I kept them for 4-5 mins on the baking tray and then transferred them into a wire rack to cool off evenly) **Tip:** A dough scraper might help you lift them off without deforming them.
